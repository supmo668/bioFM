## 1. The H4 pass is conditioned on the error it is tested against — VERIFIED

- **Code.** After each round the loop puts the round's held-out error into the next round's context
  (`src/perturb_eval/agentic_lifecycle/loop.py:483-486`, `last_msd`). The Architect prompt prints it as "Prior round
  MSD" (`llm_agent_pool.py:149-156`); every other role is given the whole context (`llm_agent_pool.py:170-175`).
  Round 0 has no such context.
- **Consequence.** `1 − ΔC` is confidence after seeing held-out error minus confidence before. H4 tests it against
  the final round's value of that same error.
- **Not disclosed.** A search of `paper.tex`, `results.tex`, `experimental_setup.tex` and `PREREGISTRATION.md` for
  `last_msd`, "prior round", "previous round" and "feedback" returns nothing.
- **Exploratory check on the committed run** (task-level Spearman, seed medians; the script reproduces the committed
  H4 values 0.663 and 0.703, so it computes the same quantity):

  | Screen | n | round-0 mean confidence vs final error | round-2 mean confidence vs final error | `1 − ΔC` vs the round-1 error the agents were shown |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Adamson | 21 | −0.01 | −0.83 | 0.67 |
  | Norman | 20 | 0.21 | −0.70 | 0.82 |

  Confidence stated before any feedback carries no rank information about error. The association appears only after
  the error is shown. Round-0 confidence barely varies across tasks.
- **What it means.** H4's outcome stands as pre-registered. Its reading does not: the data support "confidence falls
  after agents are shown a large held-out error", not "the group's confidence predicts difficulty", and not a
  compute-free or pre-run signal. The preflight probe is a round-0 quantity, and the round-0 result runs against it.

## 3. Statistics (reviewer findings; not yet individually verified)

- **Implemented as registered.** The reviewer found the H1–H5 estimators match the pre-registration and found no
  arithmetic error in the committed outcomes.
- **H5 "does not transfer" overstates.** The ridge fit is on raw error with one high-leverage Adamson task; the
  bootstrap holds the fitted weights fixed, so its interval is conditional. Suggested wording: "did not meet the
  criterion; the interval is uninformative".
- **No power statement.** Criteria threshold a point estimate at n ≈ 20; intervals are printed but never read against
  the criteria.
- **"4 of 5 criteria pass" as a headline.** H1/H2 are feasibility checks on an oracle minimum, H3 is a manipulation
  check; only H4 and H5 bear on the title, one pass and one fail. No family-wise control spans the five.
- **H3.** The pooled entropy treats rounds and seeds as independent; the diversity arises mainly after rule-based
  feedback.
- **One sweep.** LLM sampling is unseeded; seed-level variation is measured and not reported; tasks share controls,
  training pool, model and prompts.
