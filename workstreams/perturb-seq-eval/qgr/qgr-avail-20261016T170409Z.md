## The rule

- A missing status refuses.
- The rule applies on both the revision and the plain path.
- The dry run does not wait.

Rationale: a published DOI cannot be fixed by replacing a file. The Figshare API documents "available" as the finished state; "ic_success" is undocumented, so treating anything other than "available" as not done is the conservative reading.
