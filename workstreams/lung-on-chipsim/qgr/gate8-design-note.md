## 2. Where the family actually lives

Every instance has the same shape, and it is structural rather than careless:

> **The claim is written in PROSE; the check is written in CODE; nothing fails when they diverge.**

- `test_a_placeholder_PREFIX_followed_by_anything_is_NOT_accounted` — the word *anything* is in the
  NAME; all four tails in the body begin with `/` or `-`.
- `_characters_at` — its docstring says it *"RAISES on anything else… a helper that guessed would
  hand back a set that looks like evidence and is not."* Measured: `(Xn){1}[A-Z]{25}` at index 1
  returns 26 uppercase letters where the truth is `n`, silently, and that flips a disjointness
  verdict. The helper written to stop the family commits it, in the manner its own docstring forbids.
- the recorded column limit — stated against the PAD, 38 characters short of the LINE.
- the strict xfail — reason string said nine code points, the loop reached one.
- `bracket.py` docstring — TYPES "1,028 / 44 / 892" in the same change set as clause (f)'s
  *"counts are regenerated by the harness, never typed."*
- `"copy it again and tests/test_bracket.py fails"` — true only inside one directory.

In each case the prose is the thing a reader (human or model) believes, and the prose is the one
artefact the suite cannot evaluate. Patching the six does not touch the mechanism that produced them.

## 3. What r2.50 should change (design, not repairs)

1. **A quantifier in a test name must be derived, not asserted.** Any name or message containing
   *every / anything / all / N* is backed by a parametrisation whose cardinality comes from the
   artefact it quantifies over. The nine break characters were right only because the set was
   enumerated from `str.splitlines()`; the four tails were wrong because the set was hand-written.
2. **Every exclusion predicate carries a FALSE-EXCLUSION test.** Already proposed in the r2.49b
   draft and now independently justified: three of gate 8's worst findings are exclusions that
   silently removed real sites (alphanumeric tail, empty file, directory/gitlink). A false exclusion
   reads exactly like a clean result, which is why no differential caught any of them.
3. **A helper that promises to raise must be PROVEN to raise.** `_characters_at`'s contract needs a
   property test over regex constructs (counted groups, `MIN_REPEAT`, anchors, lookarounds,
   `SUBPATTERN`), not a hand-maintained list of node types. The hole was in the arm that *looked*
   handled.
4. **A derivation must consume the whole artefact.** The proof reads `.pattern` and ignores
   `.flags`; the file's own text says 640 lines earlier that *"an IGNORECASE compile has an identical
   `.pattern`"*. Derivations take the compiled object, or assert the flags they assume.
5. **The instrument is inside the scope it enforces.** `bracket.py` writes shared-stash commit
   subjects from other workstreams, untracked filenames and host paths verbatim into a TRACKED
   evidence file, with the production detectors never run over what it is about to write. Verified:
   gate 7's committed artifact carries a `perturb-seq-eval` stash subject; gate 8's is clean **by
   luck, not by a control**. Any self-check must run over the instrument's own output.

## 4. Scope recommendation, stated plainly

The three deferred clause-(e) requirements should NOT be implemented as drafted. `in_scope_unaccounted`
is 607 today and reaches 0 only via a fixture `PROVENANCE.md` the principal must author plus marker
subtraction across 171 sites. That is more machinery on top of machinery whose existing claims just
failed nine times over.

**Recommendation:** r2.50 spends its single revision on §3 items 1-3 — the mechanisms that make a
claim checkable — and the unaccounted-shape guard descopes to the in-scope Python/prose sites, with
the five excluded properties and their counts recorded as a stated limitation in the Stage 1
registered report. That is the #457 gate-9 endpoint, reached one gate early and deliberately, rather
than after another revision spent proving the same point a third time.

The alternative — implement requirements 4 and 5 as drafted, then gate 9 — is the option I would
take only if the CTO judges that §3's mechanisms are cheap enough to land alongside them. I do not
think they are, and gate 8 is the evidence.

## 5. What is NOT in doubt

Clause (f) is fully conformant on all five items, independently swept. Clause (g)'s deletion half is
done and its two-layer pin caught my own bad pin. The (e)-3 deferral was ruled an honest partial,
declared in four places including the evidence JSON. The bracket held. The failure is not that
nothing works; it is that the claims outrun what has been shown to work, and the rate is not falling:
gate 6 three survivors, gate 7 five, gate 8 forty-six findings with three defects found nine times.
