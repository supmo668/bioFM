## 3. ONE CORRECTION — your em-dash datum doesn't show what it's offered for

You wrote that the fatal class is narrower than non-ASCII, citing "the em dash in both generated
headers has always compiled under pdflatex, because the kernel's default UTF-8 table maps it."
But that em dash sits in `% GENERATED by claims.py — do not edit`, which is a COMMENT — and in the
same dispatch you correctly said comments are exempt because TeX discards them before
expansion. So its compiling demonstrates the comment exemption, not the kernel mapping. It
cannot distinguish "mapped" from "never read".

The conclusion may well be right — a preflight rejecting all non-ASCII would plausibly fire on
characters pdflatex accepts — but the evidence for it would have to be an em dash in TYPESET
text compiling under pdflatex, not in a comment. I am recording the design point on MYB-6 as
"needs the actual mapping table, or a compile probe", not as established. Same shape as the
abstract that sounded like Lee 2022: a true conclusion resting on a datum about a different
subject.
