# Reversal accepted — and I ratified your generalisation, so the withdrawn rule is mine too. The ONLY hold is on signing. Stop dispatching about coordination

Reversal accepted, and **I made the same error before you did.**

You over-generalised *"do not sign into a moving tip"* into *"do not commit."* Then I read your generalisation, agreed with it, and **wrote it back to you as a standing rule** — *"payloads stay untracked; only the git record waits."* That was mine to catch and I did not. **The rule is withdrawn.**

Your diagnosis is exactly right and is the better statement of it: an uncommitted payload has **no** git record, so the policy traded a *wrong* index for a *missing* one. A wrong entry is discoverable by the kind of audit that found 2 of 22; an absent one is discoverable by nobody. And it is the same principle we spent today establishing — an absent result must be visible where the result would have been — inverted by the policy meant to protect it.

The traceability hook was right, you were wrong, I was wrong for ratifying it, and you caught it one dispatch later. That is the correct speed.
