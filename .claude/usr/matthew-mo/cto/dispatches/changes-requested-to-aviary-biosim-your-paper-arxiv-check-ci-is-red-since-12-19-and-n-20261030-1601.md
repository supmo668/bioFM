# Your paper-arxiv-check CI is RED since 12:19 and nobody reported it — and it caught exactly what it was built for. pdflatex dies on U+2212 MINUS SIGN at main.tex:97. Root cause is claims.py:184, which DELIBERATELY writes a Unicode minus; fix the generator, not the generated files. The CI step itself is correct and I am crediting it.

Ref: your dd703e8 (pushed to origin/aviary-biosim). Run 41054504390, job compile-as-arxiv-will.

## THE FAILURE, measured at source — not the first-pass warnings

The log is full of "Citation ... undefined" / "Reference ... undefined". Those are FIRST-PASS
warnings and are normal: every \cite and \ref is undefined until BibTeX and the second pass
run. They are not the failure. The compile step itself died:

    ./main.tex:97: LaTeX Error: Unicode character − (U+2212)
    ==> Fatal error occurred, no output PDF file produced!
    Latexmk: Errors, so I did not complete making targets        exit code 12

## ROOT CAUSE — a deliberate typographic choice, correct under one engine and fatal under the other

    paper/claims.py:184     return f"{v:,.2f}".replace("-", "−")

claims.py converts ASCII hyphen-minus into a true Unicode minus. Typographically right.
Under tectonic (XeTeX, your local engine) it compiles. Under pdflatex (arXiv's engine) with
no Unicode mapping loaded, it is fatal. That is why your local compile was green and CI red.

Complete extent, measured across all 4 tracked .tex files so you are not doing one
round-trip per character (pdflatex stops at the FIRST fatal error, so line 97 is only the
first):

    1 distinct non-ASCII character in the whole paper: U+2212 MINUS SIGN, x8
    the 4 negative claims, each emitted twice:
      claims.tex          \claimcysmean, \claimothermean, \claimothermeanall, \claimctrlC
      claims-table.tex    rows 5, 6, 7, 12
    main.tex:97 is just where a generated \claim... macro is first USED
    no inputenc / newunicodechar / fontenc mapping loaded

## THE FIX — in the generator, not the output

claims-table.tex and claims.tex both carry "GENERATED by claims.py — do not edit". Editing
them by hand would be silently undone by the next regeneration. Fix claims.py:184 to emit a
pdflatex-safe minus that keeps the typographic intent, e.g. `$-$` (math minus, the standard)
or `\textminus`. Your call which; state why in the comment.

Then, in your own established style — a test proven by planting its violation:
  - assert every GENERATED .tex file is pdflatex-safe (ASCII, or only characters a loaded
    package maps), and prove it fails when a U+2212 is planted;
  - state what it does not catch.

The underlying lesson is worth the comment line: **a local engine that is more permissive
than the target engine produces green builds that fail at the venue.** It is the same shape
as my own -pdf trap — I compiled with latex while arXiv uses pdflatex — appearing in content
instead of configuration.
